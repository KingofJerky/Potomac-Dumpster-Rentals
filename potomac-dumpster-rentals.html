Our Story

BUILT IN
POTOMAC.
FOR POTOMAC.

Potomac Dumpster Rentals was founded by Jim and Carol Haskell in 2014 after Jim spent a decade driving for a national waste company and noticed something: the big guys didn't actually care about homeowners.

Jim knew every street in Potomac, Great Falls, and Bethesda. He knew the tight driveways on River Road, the strict HOAs in some neighborhoods, and exactly what size dumpster would fit without blocking a mailbox or scratching pavers.

That local knowledge is still the foundation of everything we do. Same trucks. Same family. Same commitment to getting it right.

05
Common Questions

GOT
QUESTIONS?

Standard rentals include 7 days. Need longer? We offer extended rental periods at a flat daily rate with no hassle. Just call us and we'll extend it — most customers need 3–5 days for cleanouts and 7–14 for renovations.
Hazardous materials including paint, solvents, chemicals, asbestos, and certain electronics are prohibited by Maryland law. Tires and mattresses require a small additional fee. We'll walk you through what's allowed when you call — no surprises at pickup.
If the dumpster is on private property — your driveway, backyard — no permit required. If you need it on a public street or in Montgomery County right-of-way, we handle the permit application for you. Just let us know when booking.
Quick guide: 10 yd = small cleanout or 1 room renovation. 15–20 yd = large garage, basement, or multi-room reno. 30–40 yd = full construction site or commercial job. When in doubt, call us — we'll ask 3 questions and give you an honest answer, not the biggest upsell.
We serve all of Montgomery County including Potomac, Bethesda, Rockville, Gaithersburg, North Potomac, Chevy Chase, Silver Spring, and surrounding areas. We also service parts of Loudoun County and Northern Virginia. Call to confirm your location.
