Our Story

Built in Potomac.
For Potomac.

Potomac Dumpster Rentals was founded by Jim and Carol Haskell in 2014, after Jim spent a decade in waste logistics and grew tired of watching national companies treat fine properties as afterthoughts.

Jim knew Potomac's streets intimately — the long driveways off River Road, the gated estates in Avenel, the exacting requirements of the area's homeowners associations. He knew that a crew that did not understand this neighbourhood had no business working in it.

That knowledge, and the standards that come with it, remain the foundation of everything we do. Same family. Same commitment. A service worthy of the properties we serve.

Common Questions

A few things
worth knowing

We believe an informed client is a satisfied one. If your question is not answered here, simply call — we are available six days a week.

Standard rentals include seven days. Extended periods are available at a straightforward daily rate — simply let us know and we will arrange it without fuss. Most residential clients require three to five days; renovation projects typically run seven to fourteen.
Maryland regulations prohibit hazardous materials — solvents, chemicals, asbestos, and certain electronics. Tyres and mattresses carry a modest supplemental fee. We are happy to walk through any questions about specific materials when you call, so there are no surprises on collection day.
Containers placed on private property — your driveway or grounds — require no permit. Should you require placement on a public road or within Montgomery County right-of-way, we manage the permit application on your behalf. Simply inform us at the time of booking.
A 10-yard container suits a single room clearance or modest cleanout. Fifteen to twenty yards accommodates a garage, basement, or multi-room renovation. Thirty to forty yards serves full construction sites or large-scale estate clearances. When uncertain, call us — we will advise honestly and without directing you toward a larger option than your project requires.
We serve all of Montgomery County, including Potomac, Bethesda, Chevy Chase, Rockville, North Potomac, Gaithersburg, Kensington, and Silver Spring. We also serve parts of Northern Virginia and Loudoun County. Please call to confirm availability for your address.
